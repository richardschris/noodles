Title: About
Slug: about

I've gone around eating noodles in NYC for years, but never properly documented my noodle adventures. This is a rolling record of what noodles I've eaten, my opinions, what I ordered, and what I paid. If anyone gains value from it beyond me, that's great! But it's mostly for me and my friends to know what noodles are good.

A 5 is a perfectly average, tasty noodle.
